Frequently asked

Who do I pay when I buy a book?

You pay the seller directly, using the account details shown at checkout. Codex never touches that money.

What's a scratch code for?

It's a second reference, alongside your tracking number, that a seller can ask for to confirm your order is genuine.

Can I return a book?

Raise it with the seller directly from the Track tab's complaint form — they handle returns for their own listings.

How do ads work?

Sellers pay Codex directly to promote a listing. Ads only go live after Codex confirms the payment.
